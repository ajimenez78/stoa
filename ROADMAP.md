# Roadmap

Stoa is currently an early-stage project. The roadmap is intentionally lightweight and will evolve based on user feedback, technical constraints, and whether the project demonstrates enough value to justify further investment.

## 1. Closed Testing

The current focus is validating the core experience with a small group of users.

Goals:

- Validate the main gameplay loop
- Understand whether gamification encourages real Stoic practice
- Identify usability issues and friction points
- Collect qualitative feedback from early testers

## 2. Feedback and Bug Fixing

Before expanding the product, the priority is to improve the stability and usability of the existing experience.

Focus areas:

- Fix bugs identified during testing
- Improve onboarding and navigation
- Refine unclear interactions
- Improve overall stability and performance
- Reassess product assumptions based on user feedback

## 3. Internationalization

The current content is primarily available in Spanish.

The next step is to prepare the application for additional languages, starting with English.

This includes:

- Externalizing user-facing text
- Supporting multiple locales
- Adapting content where direct translation may not be sufficient
- Preparing the content model for future language expansion

## 4. Visual Improvements and Artwork

Once the core experience is validated, the visual layer can be improved.

Possible improvements include:

- More consistent artwork and visual identity
- Improved environments and characters
- UI refinement
- Better visual feedback for progression and achievements
- Additional animations and polish

## 5. Architecture Evolution

The MVP was deliberately built with a relatively simple architecture in order to reduce complexity and iterate quickly.

The architecture will only evolve when real product or technical requirements justify it.

Potential areas of evolution include:

- Clearer separation between domain, presentation, and infrastructure concerns
- Improved persistence and synchronization
- Extraction of selected capabilities into backend services where appropriate
- Better support for cross-device experiences
- Improved observability and operational tooling
- Reviewing deployment, scalability, and cost trade-offs

The goal is not to move toward distributed systems by default, but to introduce additional architectural complexity only where it provides clear value.

## 6. More Content

If the core experience proves useful, the product can expand with additional content.

Possible additions include:

- New Stoic missions
- Additional locations and scenarios
- New exercises and practices
- More journal prompts
- Progression mechanics
- Additional mini-games
- Longer-term challenges and journeys

## Exploring

Some ideas are intentionally being kept in an exploratory stage until the core product has been validated.

Examples include:

- AI-assisted guidance
- Personalized recommendations
- Adaptive missions based on user progress
- Richer progression systems
- Social or community features
- Additional platforms
- Deeper analytics around habit formation and engagement

None of these are commitments yet. They will only move into the roadmap if user feedback and product evidence justify them.
